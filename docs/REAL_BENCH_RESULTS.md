# Real-data benchmark results

Generated by `python -m bench.real.run_real` from the Vehicle Energy Dataset (VED, Apache-2.0) following `docs/DATASET_USECASES.md`. Do not edit by hand; rerun to refresh.

```
python -m bench.real.run_real --radius 500 --households 6 --calibrated-ratio 0.5 --export fixtures/real --report docs/REAL_BENCH_RESULTS.md
```

## Data

* 32,552 real trips from 384 cars, Nov 2017 - Nov 2018, Ann Arbor MI.
* 74 cars have >= 150 trips over >= 20 active weeks and are replayed one car, one driver.
* 6 two-driver households composed from pairs of those cars, with synthetic utterances, traps and settings placed at real trip times.
* Places: 156 shared places at a 500 m radius (VED fogs trip ends; the head unit uses 150 m on clean GPS).
* Times: VED's clock is UTC; converted to America/Detroit.

## Caveats

* VED logs only some of each car's trips (median car: about 2-3 logged trips a week), so routine ratios are lower than a head unit that sees every trip would measure. Held-out checks only count weekdays on which the car was logged.
* Trip ends are fogged to major intersections; one real place can split into neighbouring clusters.
* The U9 swap rate comes from NHTS 2022 (`data/nhts/sharing_2022.json`): the share of weekday home-based-work trips in a shared household car driven by someone other than its main driver.
* Households are composed: B's real trips are re-homed to A's home. Overlapping trips are dropped.
* Settings values (temperature, seat, media, fan) are synthetic; the outside temperature and trip times that drive them are real.
